# Anime Blue GFX - research notes

## Direction

- Keep one dominant subject and make the eye land there first.
- Use cool blue layers for sky, haze, clouds and distant shapes to create depth.
- Add a blue/cyan rim light behind the character so the silhouette separates from the background.
- Use a small warm rose accent against the blue palette to echo the supplied hair and keep the image lively.
- Keep text secondary: short, high-contrast labels placed in open negative space.

## Sources consulted

- Graphic Design Analysis of the Nickelodeon Animated Film Poster Plankton: The Movie (2025): blue backlight and contrast can direct the eye toward the main character.
- Multimodal Discourse Analysis of Studio Ghibli Movie Posters: prominence, framing and information value establish hierarchy between visual and title.
- Society Structures from Anime-Cyberpunk to Postcyberpunk: foreground, middleground and background layers create spatial depth in animation.
- Bokeh Effects on the SPU: depth of field and focus are useful for directing attention to a character or object.

The final composition applies these principles to the uploaded transparent character image rather than copying any reference layout.
